The PDFs are unchanged public author, journal or repository copies. They were checked for a PDF signature, readable page structure, and successful pdfinfo inspection; SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `Paper_Manifest.json`. Not every page of every paper was read. The notes below distinguish targeted inspection from supporting references and unavailable leads.

Downloaded versions may differ from final journal publications. File-year labels identify the cited publication or initial preprint; the version field records an arXiv version when visible in the downloaded PDF.

**Private research library:** original copyright notices remain in place. Public accessibility is not permission to redistribute these PDFs in the Qnet source package or app. P24 is explicitly marked preliminary/not for distribution. No paywall, login, or download protection was bypassed.
